I've been thinking about everything we were, and I realise how much of it I took for granted.

The buses, trains and flights. The premieres, first-day-first-shows and fan shows. The street food we had to try, the restaurants we had to find. The pickups and drop-offs. The early morning calls, midnight FaceTimes and those ridiculous pin-to-pin updates.

The arguments where sometimes one of us simply let the other win.

The plans that were supposed to be for one night but somehow became much longer.

Even the little things—the moods I somehow knew, the things you needed, the packs I would order without you having to ask.

None of it felt extraordinary while we were living it.
